Small language models are a practical choice for edge devices, but a deployed model still has to fit changing memory, latency and energy budgets. Storing a separate compressed model for each budget multiplies storage, and switching between them reloads weights on a shared-memory device. Kalra and Sapra~\cite{kalra2025elastoformer} proposed nested elastic configurations for vision transformers, where smaller configurations are prefixes of one shared model. We extend this idea to decoder-only language models.
